Emergency First-Aid Protocols

Standard medical guidelines with verified sources.

Wound Bleeding / रक्तस्राव

Read the guideline: British Red Cross Read the guideline: Mayo Clinic

1. Apply direct firm pressure with sterile gauze.
2. Elevate wound above heart level.
3. Source: British Red Cross: Bleeding heavily.

Breathing Difficulty / सांस की तकलीफ

Read the guideline: MedlinePlus Read the guideline: British Red Cross

1. Sit upright and loosen tight clothing around neck.
2. Use prescribed inhaler if diagnosed with asthma.
3. Source: MedlinePlus: Difficulty breathing, first aid.
